## Before the first post

1. Switch @ritmorunning.run to a Business account (Instagram: Settings, Account type and tools, Switch to professional account, Business). Business Suite can only schedule for professional accounts.
2. Connect it to a Facebook Page. Business Suite manages Instagram through a Page. Create a simple "Ritmo Running" Page if you do not have one, then in Instagram go to Settings, Accounts Centre, and link the Page.
3. Upload `profile-photo.png`, set the name and bio from `bio.md`, and add the link once you have decided what it points to.
4. Post the four highlight covers as stories and save them as highlights (steps in `bio.md`).

## Schedule a post in Meta Business Suite (free)

Do this on a computer at business.facebook.com. The phone app works too, but carousels and alt text are easier on desktop.

1. Open business.facebook.com and pick the Ritmo Running business at the top left.
2. In the left menu, choose **Content** (or **Planner**), then **Create post**.
3. Under **Post to**, tick the **Instagram** account @ritmorunning.run. Untick Facebook unless you want to post there too.
4. Under **Media**, choose **Add photo**, then **Upload from desktop**. Pick the file(s) for that date from `posts/`.
   - For a carousel, select both slides. Check the order: `-1` must come first. Drag to reorder if needed.
5. Paste the caption for that post from `captions.md`. Add the hashtags as the last paragraph, with one blank line before them.
6. Add the alt text: hover over the image in the media area, choose the edit (pencil) or **Accessibility** option, and paste the alt text from `captions.md`. For carousels, add alt text to each slide. If you cannot find the field in Business Suite, add the alt text after publishing in the Instagram app: open the post, tap the three dots, Edit, Edit alt text.
7. Check the preview on the right: the image should show at 4:5 with no cropping. If Business Suite shows a crop control, choose **Original** or **4:5**.
8. At the bottom, open the arrow next to **Publish** and choose **Schedule**. Set the date from the table above and the time (for example 07:30). Check that the time zone shown is yours.
9. Click **Schedule**. The post now appears in **Planner** on that date.
10. Repeat for all 12 posts. You can schedule all of them today. Business Suite allows scheduling up to 75 days ahead, and the last post (1 January 2027) is 57 days away.

## After each post

- Reply to comments in the first hour, in the same calm voice.
- Share the post to your story with a short line, and add it to the matching highlight (features to Features, coach's notes to Training tips, privacy to Privacy, coming soon posts to Coming soon).
- Once a week, check Planner to confirm the next posts are still scheduled. Business Suite occasionally drops a scheduled post after an account or password change.
