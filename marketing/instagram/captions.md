## 03 · Tuesday 17 November 2026 · Coach's note: consistency

**File:** `posts/03-2026-11-17-consistency.png`

**Caption**

The run you repeat matters more than the run you remember.

One big session feels good. It is the steady weeks that change you: three or four runs, most of them easy, done again the following week. Fitness builds from that repetition, not from one heroic day.

If a week goes wrong, do not try to make it up. Pick up the next planned session and carry on. An ordinary week you can repeat is worth more than a perfect one you cannot.

## 07 · Monday 7 December 2026 · Coach's note: easy days

**File:** `posts/07-2026-12-07-easy-days.png`

**Caption**

Easy days are where the engine is built.

Most of your weekly running should feel comfortable: slow enough to talk in full sentences. That is the pace that builds your aerobic base, keeps your legs fresh and leaves energy for the harder sessions that matter.

If your easy run feels too easy, you are probably doing it right. Leave the speed for the days that call for it.

## 09 · Thursday 17 December 2026 · Coach's note: rest

**File:** `posts/09-2026-12-17-rest-days.png`

**Caption**

Rest is part of the plan, not a break from it.

Training is a stress. Getting fitter happens afterwards, while you recover. Skip the recovery and the work has nowhere to go.

So treat rest days like sessions: plan them, keep them, and do not feel you have to earn them. A short walk or some light mobility is fine. Coming back fresh to the next run is the goal.
