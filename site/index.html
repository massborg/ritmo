Every morning

Open the app. Know exactly what to do, and why.

No grids of numbers to decode. Today shows one session: what it is, how long, and the reason it is in your plan. Tap for the steps, paces or effort, and heart rate zones.

  • Short on time? Ask for a shorter version of the same session.
  • Ill, sore or busy? Tell Ritmo and the week rearranges itself.
  • Missed a session? It is moved or repeated, never piled on.

How it works

Three steps, then it keeps up with you.

  1. 1

    Tell Ritmo about you

    A short setup: your running so far, your goal, the days you can train and any injuries. If a goal is too much too soon, Ritmo says so kindly and suggests a safer one.

  2. 2

    Run, then log it

    Bring in your history from Garmin, FIT, GPX or TCX files, or log a run by hand in seconds. Automatic Garmin sync is coming, pending Garmin's approval, and Apple Health and Health Connect come with the phone apps.

  3. 3

    Your plan adapts

    After every run you see what went well and one thing to change. Your next sessions shift to match your fitness, fatigue and how the run felt.

Honest numbers

Ritmo tells you what it knows, and what it is guessing.

Paces, race predictions and fitness scores only appear when your runs support them, and each one says what it is based on. New runners train by effort until Ritmo has learned enough to set paces.

New to running

From walking to 30 minutes of running.

A gentle run/walk path, three days a week with rest days in between. Each step builds on the last, and if a session felt very hard, Ritmo holds you at that level a little longer. No watch needed.

When you can run for 30 minutes, a short time trial unlocks paces, race predictions and plans for 5K, 10K and beyond.

Training for a race

5K to marathon, built around race day.

Structured, not rigid

Base, build, peak and taper, with easy, tempo, threshold, interval, hill and long runs where they belong. Sessions move when life does.

Race readiness

A predicted finish time from your recent performances, and how ready you are for your goal, updated as you train.

Load and recovery

Fitness, fatigue and form from your training load, plus a daily readiness check that eases the plan when you need it.

Feedback on every run

Pacing, heart rate and how it felt, turned into what went well and one clear thing to change next time.

Privacy and safety

Your health data is yours.

  • Stored in the EU and private to your account.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising.
  • Garmin data is never used to train AI models.
  • The AI coach is optional, and only with your permission.
  • Export everything or delete your account at any time.
  • A health check before you start, for adults 18 and over.

Ritmo gives coaching guidance, not medical advice.

Questions

When does Ritmo launch?

The phone apps are in final testing. Join the waitlist and you will hear first. You can already try the web app.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced at launch. People on the waitlist will hear about it first.

Do I need a running watch?

No. You can log runs by hand with time, distance and how they felt. A watch or phone with GPS and heart rate gives Ritmo more to learn from.

Does it work with Garmin?

Today you can import your full Garmin history, or single FIT, GPX and TCX files. Automatic Garmin sync is coming once Garmin approves access.

Apple Health and Health Connect?

Yes, in the iPhone and Android apps, with your permission.

I have never run before. Is it for me?

Yes. The beginner path starts with short run/walk intervals and builds slowly, after a short health check.

What happens to my data?

It is stored in the EU, private to you and never sold. You can export or delete it at any time. Read the privacy notice.
