# Prompt for Claude in Chrome: schedule Ritmo cards in Meta Business Suite

Before you start:
1. Unzip `ritmo-instagram-100-cards.zip` into your Downloads folder, so the cards are in `Downloads/ritmo-instagram-100-cards/png/`.
2. Open https://business.facebook.com in Chrome and check that both the Ritmo Facebook Page and @ritmorunning.run appear at the top left.
3. Open Claude in Chrome, paste the prompt below, and change the two card numbers on the first line for each batch.

Meta only lets you schedule a limited time ahead (roughly two months), so work in batches of about 8 cards:
cards 1-8 in late December 2026, 9-16 in late February 2027, 17-24 in late April 2027, and so on. `calendar.md` has every date.

---

```
Schedule Ritmo Running Instagram cards 1 to 8 in Meta Business Suite, one post per card.

Where things are:
- Card images: on my computer in Downloads/ritmo-instagram-100-cards/png/ (file names start with card-001, card-002 ...).
- Caption, hashtags, alt text and date for every card: https://github.com/massborg/ritmo/blob/marketing/marketing/instagram/cards/captions.md
  Open that page in a new tab and read the section for each card (sections are headed "## 001 ·", "## 002 ·" and so on).

For each card, in number order:
1. In Meta Business Suite, go to Planner and click Create post.
2. Under "Post to", select both the Facebook Page and the Instagram account @ritmorunning.run.
3. Add the image: the PNG whose file name starts with that card's number. If you cannot open my files, stop and ask me to attach the image, then carry on.
4. Text: paste the card's Caption exactly as written, then a blank line, then the card's Hashtags line. Do not add, remove or change any words, and do not add emoji.
5. Alt text: open the accessibility or alt text option for the image and paste the card's Alt text exactly.
6. Schedule: choose Schedule (not Publish now) and set the card's "Suggested date" at 07:30, Malta time (Europe/Malta). Check that the time zone shown is Malta's; if it is a different zone, tell me before scheduling.
7. Click Schedule and wait until the post shows in the Planner on the right date before moving to the next card.

Rules:
- Never click "Publish now". Never boost a post or start an ad, and never enter payment details.
- If Meta does not allow a date that far ahead, stop and tell me the last card you scheduled.
- If anything looks different from these steps, or a button would spend money or post immediately, stop and ask me.

When you are done, give me a short list: card number, date and time scheduled, and whether it went to Instagram, Facebook or both.
```
